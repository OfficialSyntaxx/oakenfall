The plain truth

Privacy

Oakenfall collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind — not in the game, and not on this website. What follows is the whole of it, in plain words.

Short version: your hold never leaves your device unless you personally tap a button to send a bug report. Everything else — the map, your settlers, your chronicle, your unlocks — is stored on your own phone or computer and nowhere else.

What stays on your device

🏰 Your holds

All four save slots — the map, your settlers and their whole history, the chronicle, your coins and research — are written to your device's own storage. On a phone that is the app's private storage; in a browser it is that browser's local storage for this site. None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, or readable by us. Clearing the app's data, or the site's data in your browser, deletes it permanently.

🎁 Unlock codes

A cosmetic pack you buy comes back as a code. The game checks it on your device, against a key built into the game itself, and never contacts a server to do it — redeeming works with the aeroplane mode on. We do not learn that you redeemed it, and the game holds no record of who you are.

⚙️ Your settings

Sound, music, whether the store row is expanded, which slot you played last. Local, small, and yours.

The one thing that leaves your device

If you tap 🐛 Report Bug or 💡 Suggest inside the game and then send it, that report is filed as an issue in Oakenfall's public issue tracker on GitHub. Nothing is sent unless you press the button. Because that tracker is public, please treat everything in the box as public too.

A report contains what you typed, plus a short technical note the game assembles so a fault can be understood without a conversation:

  • the game version, mode, map size and the day your hold has reached;
  • your population, buildings, coins, stores and completed research;
  • your device's browser identification string and your screen size;
  • any recent errors the game caught while running.

That is the complete list, and you can read it yourself before sending — it is shown in the box. It contains no name, no email address, no location and no identifier for you or your device. If sending fails, the game copies the report to your clipboard instead so you can decide what to do with it.

The 📷 photograph and 🪪 share card buttons hand an image to your own device's sharing menu. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice; it does not pass through us.

This website

This site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps ordinary server logs — the requests made and the addresses they came from — as any web host does. We do not add analytics on top of that, and there is nothing here that follows you between sites. Every font, script and image is served from this domain; the game asks nothing of a third party, which is also why it plays perfectly well with no connection at all.

Children

Oakenfall is suitable for all ages and is built the same way for everyone. Since it gathers no personal information from anybody, there is nothing collected from a child either.

If this changes

Any change to what the game does with your data will be written into the Chronicle alongside the release that makes it, and this page will be updated to match. Nothing described here can change for a version you have already installed.

Asking us something

Questions, or a request about data, can go to the Tavern, where the project's contact routes are listed. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on your behalf — but if you want an issue you filed removed from the public tracker, say so and it will be.
